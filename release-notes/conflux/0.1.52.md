# Ars Conflux 0.1.52

- Added a dedicated **Projectile Replacement** glyph category between forms and augments. Existing Conflux projectile replacements are classified automatically.
- Projectile replacements use charcoal Ars glyph frames. On 1.21.1, the category icon reuses the augment symbol with a charcoal background; its color is configurable with `glyph_icons.projectile_color`.
- Fixed native spell-wheel previews for replacement payloads, including **Self → Orbit → Ball Lightning**. Later payloads no longer overwrite an earlier effect's primary preview.
- Applied category separation to ordinary spell books and the optional Ars Zero staff catalog. The 1.20.1 book retains its native text-heading layout.
- Existing spell recipes, glyph IDs, validation and casting behavior are unchanged. No addon update is needed to recognize existing `ProjectileForm` glyphs.

Available for Minecraft **1.21.1 NeoForge** and **1.20.1 Forge**. Targeted unit tests and a 1.21.1 client preview with Iron & Nouveau, NEG, Elemental and Zero passed; both release packages were checked, including Forge remapping.

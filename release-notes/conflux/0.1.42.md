# Ars Conflux 0.1.42

- Add **Confluence Spell Power**, a shared percentage damage attribute: `ars_conflux:confluence_spell_power`.
- Add optional per-school percentage power attributes. General power also works for spells with no declared school. General and weighted school bonuses add together before the final multiplier is clamped to zero.
- Support negative values: `0.2` means +20%, and `-0.2` means -20%. All new bonuses default to **0**; this update grants no free progression or equipment bonuses.
- Add optional weighted schools, datapack definitions, per-glyph assignments and target damage rules. No schools or glyph assignments are preset. Definitions, assignments and target rules support `/reload`.
- Register custom school power attributes with `schools.power_attributes` in `ars_conflux-startup.toml`. Attribute registration requires a restart and matching client/server lists. Target damage rules remain separately disabled by default.
- Unify percentage damage calculation across supported Ars and native provider paths, avoiding duplicate scaling when both dispatch Ars damage events.
- Preserve caster attribution and per-effect school snapshots in shared executions, delayed payloads and native emitters. Ordinary attacks and ordinary summoned creatures are not globally turned into spell damage.
- Keep healing, costs, durations and native provider stats separate from the new percentage damage bonuses.

Iron & Nouveau **0.17.33** and Goety & Nouveau **0.1.6** include their provider-specific lifecycle updates and require Conflux **0.1.42** or newer.

Available for Minecraft **1.21.1 NeoForge** and **1.20.1 Forge**.

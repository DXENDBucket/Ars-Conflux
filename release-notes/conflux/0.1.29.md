# Ars Conflux 0.1.29

- Add optional integration with Ars 'n Spells 3.4.0 carriers: exported scrolls, transcribed items and weapons, and Ars spells bound to native Iron spellbooks.
- Route these casts through Conflux's shared preparation and cooldown handling. Addon casting times, movement restrictions and cooldown settings continue to apply.
- Wait for preparation before dispatching the spell or committing addon cooldowns. Repeated use during preparation keeps the existing session instead of creating extra casts.
- Cancel or revalidate pending casts when the caster changes equipment, spell data, dimension or casting state. Casting from a Curios spellbook also checks that the original book remains unchanged.
- Preserve Ars 'n Spells' targeting, crafting, resource multipliers, validation, native proxy-slot cooldowns and payment lifecycle. No additional mana charge is introduced.
- Keep Ars 'n Spells and Iron optional. Compatible hooks are enabled only when the matching upstream entry points are available.

Available for Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge and Minecraft 1.20.1 / Forge.

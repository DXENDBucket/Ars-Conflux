# Ars Conflux 0.1.40

- Add shared, synchronized `ars_conflux:cooldown_reduction` and `ars_conflux:cast_time_reduction` attributes for players and other living entities.
- Both default to **0**, preserving existing timing. A value of `0.25` removes another 25% of the time remaining after a provider's native modifiers.
- Apply cast-time reduction once after the configured MAXIMUM/SUM preparation calculation, including supported Hex-Ars Linker and Ars Zero entry points.
- Apply cooldown reduction when a new cooldown is recorded through Conflux's shared cooldown storage. Existing Cataclysm, BOMD, Aquamirae, Mowzie and Legendary Monsters integrations using that storage benefit without separate addon updates.
- Snapshot timing when preparation/cooldown starts; changing gear does not rewrite an already-running timer. Result-dependent cooldown commits do not apply the reduction twice.
- Keep spell delays, channel durations, pulse intervals and action animations separate from preparation time.

Equipment or modpack progression must supply the new attributes; this update does not grant free bonuses. Iron & Nouveau **0.17.31** and Goety & Nouveau **0.1.5** connect their native timing systems to these attributes.

Available for Minecraft **1.21.1 NeoForge** and **1.20.1 Forge**.

# Cataclysm & Nouveau 0.1.2

Requires Ars Conflux 0.1.32.

Wither Smoke and Scylla Lightning Field now support AOE. Each unit changes their starting radius by one block, retaining the three-block base, native synced visuals and damage. Shrink rates adapt to the larger area while preserving the original final footprint. Radius is bounded from 0.5 to 16 blocks.

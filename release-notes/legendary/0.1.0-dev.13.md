# Legendary Monsters & Nouveau — First CurseForge release

Available for Minecraft 1.21.1 NeoForge and 1.20.1 Forge, with 30 Ars Nouveau glyphs on both platforms.

- Storm and frost abilities: Cloud Bolt, Electric Wave, Capturing Tornado, Ground Lightning, Frost Spike and Energy Beam.
- Fire Column, Fire Breath, Poisonous Shockwave and lingering Poison Field.
- Giant and Gravity Shulker Bullets.
- Soul weapons, projectiles, pillars, protection and healing effects.
- Annihilation bombs, beams, attack portals, plasma orbs, geysers and ground effects.
- Soul Javelin and Chorus Bomb.
- Native projectile/effect visuals, supported Ars augments, Ars damage growth and Conflux spell contexts.
- Trigger-based additional Mana costs, sustained ability payments and configurable cooldowns.
- Default caps for maximum-health damage components, with configuration options.
- Scribe's Table glyph recipes using Source Gems and corresponding native materials. All glyphs default to Ars Tier 1.
- English and Simplified Chinese localization.

Requires Ars Nouveau, Ars Conflux and Legendary Monsters, plus their native dependencies. Other Nouveau integration addons are optional.

The version retains its existing development-series identifier. Original mod assets are referenced at runtime, not redistributed with this addon.

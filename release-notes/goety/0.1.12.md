# Goety & Nouveau 0.1.12

- Adds quiet native summon-cap readiness hints for Blackguard and Leapleaf, helping spellcasting AI avoid selecting a summon group that is already full. Readiness checks stop at the relevant native limit instead of allocating a complete servant list.
- Adds Illusion summoning hints and lets NPC self-cast illusions inherit their caster's live hostile target. Player aiming, clone variants, native lifetimes, dismissal and resource rules remain unchanged.
- Includes real non-player Curios equipment lookup and the public NPC staff-qualification API from 0.1.10–0.1.11. Content mods may provide real equipment or explicit staff qualifications; this addon does not automatically equip mobs or grant free casting, extra resources or unlimited summons.
- Native costs, preparation, cooldowns, summon limits and mutually exclusive staff-variant precedence remain authoritative. Requires Ars Conflux 0.1.47+ within the 0.1.x series.

Available for Minecraft 1.21.1 NeoForge and 1.20.1 Forge. Both builds were compiled, packaged and checked statically, including Forge refmaps. No additional in-game test was run for this release.

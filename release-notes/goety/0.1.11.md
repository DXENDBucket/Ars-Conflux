# Goety & Nouveau 0.1.11

- Adds a public NPC staff-qualification API: grant selected Goety staff schools, selected staff identities, or all staff qualifications without modifying the caster's equipped items.
- Native school and staff-identity branches use these explicit grants. Mutually exclusive native variants still follow native branch order; normal players and unregistered NPCs keep their existing behavior.
- Native costs, preparation, cooldowns, summon caps and real equipment requirements remain authoritative. Includes the real NPC Curios lookup and allied execution-direction fixes from 0.1.10.
- Requires Ars Conflux 0.1.47 or newer in the 0.1.x series. Both loaders are built and checked statically; no in-game test is launched for this batch.

# Goety & Nouveau 0.1.6

**Requires Ars Conflux 0.1.42 or newer.**

- Support shared Confluence and optional school percentage spell-power attributes through the common damage path.
- Preserve the original caster and school snapshot in Bolting's separate delayed task.
- Keep Killing's self-damage payment separate from spell-power bonuses; legitimate self-targeted spell damage follows the shared formula.
- Retain native Goety potency, equipment modifiers, healing, timing and soul-energy payment. New percentage attributes default to **0**, and no schools are preset.

Focused NeoForge coexistence checks covered native Goety Soul Bolt, native Iron Firebolt and Goety Thunderbolt with Ars 'n Spells 3.4.0, confirming a single shared damage multiplier.

Available for Minecraft **1.21.1 NeoForge** and **1.20.1 Forge**.

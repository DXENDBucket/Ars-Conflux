# Iron & Nouveau 0.17.21

- Use native Iron spell icons as glyph centers in inventory and spell-building interfaces.
- Integrate Ars Conflux's configurable native glyph frames and center scaling.
- Keep the previous glyph appearance available through independent client configuration switches.

Requires Ars Conflux 0.1.25 or newer in the 0.1.x series.
Available for Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge and Minecraft 1.20.1 / Forge.

# Iron & Nouveau 0.17.37

**Requires Ars Conflux 0.1.45 or newer.** Available for Minecraft **1.21.1 NeoForge** and **1.20.1 Forge**.

Changes since 0.17.33:

- Fix **Thunderstorm cast onto another entity targeting and damaging its actual caster**. The recipient remains the storm's centre; the original caster retains target relations, lightning ownership, damage attribution and upkeep payment. Native lightning entities, visuals, range and damage formulas are retained.
- Keep unmanaged native Iron Thunderstorm behavior unchanged. Focused real-lightning tests passed on both game versions, including caster immunity next to a struck enemy and stopping strikes when the original mana pool is empty.
- Support Conflux's percentage mana-discount rings consistently in initial budgets, scattered shots and actual trigger payments.
- Expose native-derived cooldowns and resource-capacity information for non-player casting integrations, and expand optional glyph ability hints. Normal installation does not add casting AI to mobs.
- Include Forge compilation-cache/refmap packaging corrections.

Player resource selection, scroll-learning options, native spell levels, creative settings and summon limits retain their configured behavior. Client visuals were not manually rechecked in this update.

# Iron & Nouveau 0.17.31

**Requires Ars Conflux 0.1.40 or newer.**

- Support Conflux's shared cooldown and cast-time reduction attributes.
- Apply shared cooldown reduction after native Iron cooldown reduction, weapon modifiers and supported per-shot recast division.
- Include managed native self-casting and non-player bound-tool cooldowns.
- Use Conflux's shared preparation timing without preparing a native self-cast a second time.

Both attributes default to **0**. Existing timing is preserved until equipment, effects or modpack progression provide a bonus. Ordinary Iron casts outside this integration retain their native timing rules.

Available for Minecraft **1.21.1 NeoForge** and **1.20.1 Forge**.

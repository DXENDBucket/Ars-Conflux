# Ars Fusion — First CurseForge release

Available for Minecraft 1.21.1 NeoForge and 1.20.1 Forge.

- Fuse Ars armor with same-slot donor armor while retaining Ars appearance, threads and upgrades.
- Bind Enchanter's Sword casting to supported melee weapons and mining tools.
- Fuse bows and crossbows while preserving their native ammunition and projectile types, including supported Ars augment arrows.
- Add Enchanter's Shield block buffs and mana-funded self-repair to supported shields.
- Fuse staff attributes into Ars spellbooks, wands, mirrors and other supported Ars casting tools. Iron spellbooks are not fusion materials.
- Optional native Iron armor spell-container support and Goety staff-identity integration.
- Configurable fusion categories, automatic compatibility, item tags and attribute blacklists.
- Optional toughness based on actual Ars armor upgrade tier, disabled by default.

Use a vanilla anvil: receiving item on the left, corresponding material on the right; each fusion costs one experience level.

Ars Nouveau is required. Iron, Goety, Ars Conflux and other Nouveau integration addons are optional. When also using Iron & Nouveau, version 0.17.32 or newer is required.

MIT licensed. No native mod assets or dependency JARs are bundled.

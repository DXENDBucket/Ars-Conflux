# Mowzie & Nouveau 0.1.2

**Requires Ars Conflux 0.1.43 or newer.** Available for Minecraft **1.21.1 NeoForge** and **1.20.1 Forge**.

- Support Conflux's configurable percentage mana-discount rings.
- Calculate Burrow's additional upkeep from the original cost before applying the shared discount, avoiding double-discounting or missing augment-related upkeep.

Native ability behavior, damage, cooldowns and crafting materials remain unchanged. Distribution checks include Forge production mappings, license notices and exclusion of original mod assets or code. Client visuals were not manually rechecked for this cost calculation update.
